RODRIGUEZ NEWS KENYA
======================
This is a starter website for Rodriguez News Kenya.

To preview:
1. Keep index.html and style.css in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or another browser.

To publish online:
- Buy a domain such as rodrigueznews.co.ke (if available).
- Get web hosting.
- Upload index.html and style.css to the hosting public folder.

The news cards are placeholders. Replace them with your real stories, photos and links.
